问题现象 / 场景

  • Note: Before proceeding, have the customer be sure to back up their local data before erasing it from the device. A data erase cannot be reversed.

背景信息

  • A Data Box POD device can be reset by erasing local data from the device. To erase local data, perform the following steps:
  • In the local web UI, go to Data erase.
  • Enter the device password, and select Erase data.
  • At the confirmation prompt, select Yes to continue. A data erase can take as long as 50 minutes.
  • For detailed and updated information, go to: Erase local data from your device
