  • 关于为什么remediator花了很长时间才修复node？
  • AKS remediator的触发机制为：当node连续十分钟报告not ready的状态，或者AKS master在十分钟内，没有收到node的状态报告就会触发上述remediation，当每一个remediation event完成后，如果node依然没有恢复ready状态，则按照上述序列顺序进行，直至redeploy。而在本案例中，故障节点在通过remediation进行恢复时，都能及时恢复到ready状态，并在持续运行一段时间之后，再次变为not ready，因此redeploy的修复动作一直等到了北京时间 09/12 03:15 AM的时候才被触发，并最终修复了该node。

For CA:

AKS clusters created prior to May 2019 have certificates that expire after two years. Any cluster created after May 2019 or any cluster that has its certificates rotated have Cluster CA certificates that expire after 30 years.

All other AKS certificates, which use the Cluster CA to for signing, will expire after two years and are automatically rotated during AKS version upgrade. To verify when your cluster was created, use kubectl get nodes to see the Age of your node pools.

For other certificates:

Now we have distributed a new feature#  https://github.com/Azure/AKS/releases/tag/2021-07-15

Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS) will now rotate your intermediate certificates during a version upgrade operation.

关于为什么remediator花了很长时间才修复node？

AKS remediator的触发机制为：当node连续十分钟报告not ready的状态，或者AKS master在十分钟内，没有收到node的状态报告就会触发上述remediation，当每一个remediation event完成后，如果node依然没有恢复ready状态，则按照上述序列顺序进行，直至redeploy。而在本案例中，故障节点在通过remediation进行恢复时，都能及时恢复到ready状态，并在持续运行一段时间之后，再次变为not ready，因此redeploy的修复动作一直等到了北京时间 09/12 03:15 AM的时候才被触发，并最终修复了该node。
