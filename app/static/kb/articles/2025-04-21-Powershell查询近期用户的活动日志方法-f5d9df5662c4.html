处理工单20250401346447中测试了两个关于Powershell获取活动日志的命令，供大家后续参考。

场景一、获取订阅下哪些用户进行了开关机操作

使用Get-AzActivityLog命令获取订阅下活动日志事件，如下命令可对开关机及虚拟机更改操作进行了筛选，并输出到csv文件中

Connect-AzAccount -Environment AzureChinaCloud

$startDate = (Get-Date).AddDays(-1)

$logs = Get-AzActivityLog -StartTime $startDate

#Filter specific logs

$filteredLogs = $logs | Where-Object { $_.Authorization.Action -eq "Microsoft.Compute/virtualMachines/start/action" -or $_.Authorization.Action -eq "Microsoft.Compute/virtualMachines/deallocate/action"}

#Export to CSV File

$filteredLogs |Select-Object @{Name="Action";Expression={$_.Authorization.Action}},ResourceGroupName,ResourceId,Caller,Status | Export-Csv -Path "C:\tmp\AzureActivityLog.csv" -NoTypeInformation -Encoding UTF8

场景二、 获取某个用户过去1天在订阅下的活动日志记录并输出到csv文件中。

$userName = "zuoxiaojing@microsoftinternal.partner.onmschina.cn"

$startTime = (Get-Date).AddDays(-1)

$endTime = Get-Date

$activityLogs = Get-AzActivityLog -StartTime $startTime -EndTime $endTime | Where-Object { $_.Caller -eq $userName}

$activityLogs |Select-Object @{Name="Action";Expression={$_.Authorization.Action}},ResourceGroupName,ResourceId,Caller,Status |Export-Csv -Path "C:\tmp\AzureActivityLog.csv" -NoTypeInformation -Encoding UTF8
