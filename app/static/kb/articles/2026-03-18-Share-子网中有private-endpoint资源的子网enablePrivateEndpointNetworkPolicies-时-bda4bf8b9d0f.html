Update

Humingyue

对于子网中有private endpoint资源的子网enablePrivateEndpointNetworkPolicies 时会提示报错

但对于一个没有部署private endpoint 资源的子网是可以enable PrivateEndpointNetworkPolicies，

对于一个已经enable PrivateEndpointNetworkPolicies的子网当前无法部署private endpoint。在使用ARM 模板部署时会出现如下提醒。

这里需要注意一下：如果通过portal 部署private endpoint资源时会显示成功部署了private endpoint，但实际上portal部署时自动将PrivateEndpointNetworkPolicies配置disable了。

根据与产品团队的沟通，预计China Azure在2025/04支持在Private Endpoint所在的Subnet启用PrivateEndpointNetworkPolicies，但产品组也表示这是个大概的预期 “This is internal only, as there are a lot of reasons for possible delays. However, we have established an agreed upon timeline with the engineering team, so you should expect use some time in April.”。
