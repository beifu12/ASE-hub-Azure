背景，客户向针对于自定义日志来设置 同时满足% Committed Bytes In Use   大于  多少且 Available Mbytes 小于多少时告警

以下只是示例，请根据您的实际情况来设置测试

例如：

我为了测试方便。您可以根据实际需求来调整标黄的数值

Perf

| where ObjectName == "Memory"

| where CounterName in ("% Committed Bytes In Use", "Available Bytes")

| summarize

MaxCommittedBytes=maxif(CounterValue, CounterName == "% Committed Bytes In Use"),

MinAvailableBytes=minif(CounterValue, CounterName == "Available Bytes")

by _ResourceId, Computer, bin(TimeGenerated, 5m)

| where MaxCommittedBytes > 20 and MinAvailableBytes < 6703919104

| order by TimeGenerated desc

预览显示在CST 10:30-10:50 符合 % Committed Bytes In Use >20 且Available Bytes <6703919104
