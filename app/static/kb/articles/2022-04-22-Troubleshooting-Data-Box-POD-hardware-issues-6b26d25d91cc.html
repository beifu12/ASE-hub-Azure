问题现象 / 场景

  • Contact the customer and gather as much information as you can about the issue (e.g. what is the error message on the client, any screenshots, past changes, or any additional relevant information).

背景信息

  • What seems to be the failing component?
  • Confirm if the device is powering on. If not:
  • Power outlet not working, try a different power outlet.
  • Power cable is damaged and need to be replaced.
  • Try disconnecting all cables for at least 15 mins to fully drain power. Reconnect power cable on the device and on the outlet.
  • If different power outlet, cables and previous steps already taken and device is not powering on, send the unit back.
  • Are there amber/red led's on the unit?
  • Engage TA or raise ICM if needed.

排查思路

  • Double-check to make sure the device has been properly cabled
  • If Webui is accessible check for alerts/warnings, run diagnostics and collect support package.
  • Is the unit working to collect logs? If so collect support package and run diagnostics
