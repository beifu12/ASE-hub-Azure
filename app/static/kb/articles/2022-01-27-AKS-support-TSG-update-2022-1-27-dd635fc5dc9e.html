Vivian CVE-2021-4034 (PwnKit) - Talking Points Published

Regarding recent press stories discussing CVE-2021-4034 (PwnKit), present by default in major Linux distributions, the Microsoft Security Response Center (MSRC) monitors public vulnerability disclosures affecting open-source and 3rd party software to determine if further investigation is warranted based on the vulnerability, service, or product affected. When necessary, Microsoft will investigate and determine if our services/products are at risk from an open-source or 3rd party vulnerability, or if customer data has been impacted. If Microsoft services are at risk, mitigations will be implemented to ensure the security of our platform and customers. In the event impact to customer data is found, any impacted customer will be notified.

If by the end of our investigation you do not receive a communication from Microsoft regarding a publicly disclosed vulnerability, then it is safe to assume our services and your data, are not at risk.

We encourage our customers to practice industry-standard best practices for security and data protection including embracing the Zero Trust Security model and adopting robust strategies to manage security updates, antivirus updates, and passwords. More information on Zero Trust Security is available at https://aka.ms/zerotrust. Additional information is available at https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security.
