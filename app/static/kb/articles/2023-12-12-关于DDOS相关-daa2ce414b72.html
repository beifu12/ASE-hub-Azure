关于DDoS清洗的开启及结束流程

当平台监测到DDoS攻击并达到一定阈值后，首先会触发平台自有的清洗，攻击结束后，清洗也随之结束。

而如若攻击流量过大，超过一定阈值，后线团队会手动开启电信级别的清洗（目前运营商级别的清洗服务仅有电信，所以其他线路也会将流量迁至电信线路进行清洗）。

通常情况下，为避免DDoS反复出现，所以清洗时长默认为12/24小时。

如需终止电信层面的清洗，需要请客户提交工单（仅针对受攻击不频繁的客户）。

清洗过程中，会对所有流量进行筛选。不同于封堵，如果符合常见的异常流量条件，会被drop，而正常请求的流量通常不会受到影响。但其中可能会有误判，例如某些业务的请求会与异常流量类似。

具体流量是否被误判，还需要客户从数据帧、实际应用层面等分析。

Overview of DDoS defense in Azure China:

Azure China has deployed monitoring and defense systems against DDoS attacks, including ISP-facing plane, inbound side of datacenters, etc. And by these means, the overall health of the platform can be guaranteed.

Once a DDoS attack is detected on the backend, the system will respond automatically. The malicious traffic will be filtered and washed before entering the customer’s systems. In the meantime, the customer’s systems which are being under attack will be isolated so that it won’t interfere with the platform.

When the attack is enormous enough to have a significant impact on the platform, the system will send out alerts and manual filtering/washing on ISP level will be carried out until the attack is no longer a threat.

Sometimes when the pattern of normal traffic is similar as the malicious one, it could be blocked as well. The traffic can’t be perfectly filtered and it’s a common difficulty for all datacenters.
