###From O365 portal:

You can refer to the following 2 methods to get/update password expiration policy:

https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/admin/manage/set-password-expiration-policy?view=o365-worldwide

1 From Office 365 portal:

1.1 Login 21V Office 365 portal with your admin account: https://microsoft365.microsoftonline.cn/?auth=2

1.2 Check/Update password expiration policy as below steps, you can set a number of days from 14 to 730.

2. Using Powershell:

Connect-MsolService -AzureEnvironment AzureChinaCloud

Get-MsolPasswordPolicy -DomainName MicrosoftInternal.partner.onmschina.cn

Set-MsolPasswordPolicy -DomainName MicrosoftInternal.partner.onmschina.cn -ValidityPeriod 60 -NotificationDays 15
