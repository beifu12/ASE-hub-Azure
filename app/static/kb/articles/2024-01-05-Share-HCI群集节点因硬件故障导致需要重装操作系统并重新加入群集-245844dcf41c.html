在重新对HCI节点安装操作系统前，需要先在Failover cluster中将节点的 “角色”排出，将群集上的VM\存储等迁移到其他节点；

1，请客户对问题节点更换损坏的硬件，重新安装操作系统，配置IP地址，DNS地址

2，将节点加域：

2, 在带外配置网卡 teaming, 虚拟交换机；

New-VMSwitch -Name "MGMTVSwitch" -NetAdapterName "Ethernet 2", "Ethernet 4" - EnableEmbeddedTeaming $true -AllowManagementOS $false

Add-VMNetworkAdapter -SwitchName "MGMTVSwitch" -Name "VMGMT" -ManagementOS

New-NetIPAddress -InterfaceAlias "vEthernet (VMGMT)" -IPAddress 10.140.121.235 - PrefixLength 27 -DefaultGateway 10.140.121.224

Set-DnsClientServerAddress -InterfaceAlias "vEthernet (VMGMT)" -ServerAddresses ("192.168.199.7","192.168.199.8")

3，连接到WAC后，安装必要的角色：

4，此时可以RDP到节点上，继续执行后面的配置步骤：

New-VMSwitch -Name "SMBVSwitch" -NetAdapterName "Ethernet", "Ethernet 3" - EnableEmbeddedTeaming $true -AllowManagementOS $false

Add-VMNetworkAdapter -SwitchName "SMBVSwitch" -Name "SMB01" -ManagementOS

Enable-NetAdapterRDMA -Name "vEthernet (SMB01)"

Add-VMNetworkAdapter -SwitchName "SMBVSwitch" -Name "SMB02" -ManagementOS

Enable-NetAdapterRDMA -Name "vEthernet (SMB02)"

Set-VMNetworkAdapterVlan -VMNetworkAdapterName "SMB01" -VlanId 1290 -Access – ManagementOS

Set-VMNetworkAdapterVlan -VMNetworkAdapterName "SMB02" -VlanId 1291 -Access – ManagementOS

Set-VMNetworkAdapterTeamMapping -VMNetworkAdapterName "SMB01" - PhysicalNetAdapterName "Ethernet" -ManagementOS

Set-VMNetworkAdapterTeamMapping -VMNetworkAdapterName "SMB02" - PhysicalNetAdapterName "Ethernet 3" -ManagementOS

New-NetIPAddress -InterfaceAlias "vEthernet (SMB01)" -IPAddress 10.140.129.15 - PrefixLength 24

New-NetIPAddress -InterfaceAlias "vEthernet (SMB02)" -IPAddress 10.140.129.145 - PrefixLength 24

Set-DnsClient -InterfaceAlias "vEthernet (SMB01)" -RegisterThisConnectionsAddress $false

Set-DnsClient -InterfaceAlias "vEthernet (SMB02)" -RegisterThisConnectionsAddress $false
