2025/3/25

最近发现在portal上创建AKS集群的时候，已经不提供kubenet网络模型部署了：

但是文档上并没有说不支持kubenet模型，于是在powershell中尝试创建：

az aks create --resource-group AKS --name akstest2 --node-count 2 --network-plugin kubenet --network-policy calico --outbound-type loadBalancer --vnet-subnet-id "/subscriptions/eae551d8-92de-41a1-bef9-a74aebffad7f/resourceGroups/AKS/providers/Microsoft.Network/virtualNetworks/AKS-vnet/subnets/default2" --generate-ssh-keys

结果报错：

经过查询必须要指定auto-upgrade-channel才能创建：

az aks create --resource-group AKS --name akstest1 --node-count 2 --network-plugin kubenet --network-policy calico --auto-upgrade-channel stable --outbound-type loadBalancer --vnet-subnet-id "/subscriptions/eae551d8-92de-41a1-bef9-a74aebffad7f/resourceGroups/AKS/providers/Microsoft.Network/virtualNetworks/AKS-vnet/subnets/default2" --generate-ssh-keys
