Update Time

2021/08/23

Hi team,

Customer recently reported that they cannot find “Work or School account” option when trying to add accounts into Microsoft Authenticator app on HUAWEI phones.

We have confirmed with product team that this is an undocumented behavior change.

For Android phones that do not support Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging system and Google Play Services, “Work or School account” option will be hidden.

Please note that this behavior not only show on HUAWEI phones, but on other brand phones as well, as long as they don’t support Google’s infra services.

If customer need to add app code, please follow next steps:

In the Configure mobile app page, click the link “Configure app without notifications”, and make sure that you see Account Name and Secret Key (NOT Code and Uri).

In the Authenticator app, click “Other accounts (Google, Facebook etc.)” and scan this QR code.

We have asked product manager to update corresponding document, stay tuned.

Thanks and Best Regards,

Steve Shi
