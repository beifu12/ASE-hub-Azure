Dear all,

The following table is the latest support matrix for Azure Storage resource recovery in Mooncake, feel free to let me know if you have any questions regarding the recovery.

Recovery is only possible if the customer has deleted a container or storage account or blob and is not garbage collected. It can generally take "anytime from deletion" to 15 days for the Storage service to perform garbage collection so it is not definite that the storage objects can be recovered with an SLA.

Please inform the customer in your initial response email that Microsoft Support will try to recover the Container/Account/Blob on best effort basis and can not guarantee the recovery.
