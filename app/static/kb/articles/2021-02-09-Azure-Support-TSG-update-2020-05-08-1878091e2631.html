Known issue

Case: 2020041519872

Background:

By default, the service version of Azure Storage Account is 2009-09-19 which does not support Range: bytes=startByte-, if this kind of Storage Account is source, it’ll encounter the specified Range-Gets issue.

Solution:

If customer doesn’t want the Range-Get, please engage CDN engineering team to disable Range-Gets on Azure CDN China side and mitigate this issue.

Customer can update the service version of Azure Storage to 2011-08-18 or newer to avoid this issue.

New-AzStorageContext -StorageAccountName "<Storage Account Name>" -StorageAccountKey "<Storage Access Key>"

Update-AzStorageBlobServiceProperty -ResourceGroupName "<Resource Group Name>" -AccountName "<Storage Account Name>"  -DefaultServiceVersion 2011-08-18
