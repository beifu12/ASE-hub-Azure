Hi team，

需要在vm中执行以下脚本

sudo tee /etc/systemd/system/azuremonitor-kqlextension.service > /dev/null <<EOF

[Unit]

Description=Fake Azure Monitor KQL Extension

After=network.target

[Service]

Type=simple

ExecStart=/bin/bash -c 'echo "Fake service started"; sleep infinity'

Restart=always

[Install]

WantedBy=multi-user.target

EOF

sudo systemctl daemon-reload

sudo systemctl enable azuremonitor-kqlextension.service

原因是ubuntu上新的AMA代理检查到vm上已有AMA代理，升级时会检测 service azuremonitor-kqlextension.service

但旧版本的AMA中没有azuremonitor-kqlextension.service，导致升级时执行stop azuremonitor-kqlextension.service 报错
