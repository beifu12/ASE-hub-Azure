对于appgwv1/wafv1的containerid/nodeid可以直接通过kusto获取，但是v2的貌似是获取不了的。可以参考如下方法进行获取。

这里用创建时间去过滤，我的appgw是10/23号03:08时创建的

LogContainerSnapshot

| where (TIMESTAMP >= datetime(2019-10-23T02:00:00Z) and TIMESTAMP < datetime(2019-10-23T03:50:51Z))

| where subscriptionId == "c6a39d81-a56f-4654-a0d6-43080d8f4ee3"

| project TIMESTAMP, Tenant, Role, containerType, containerId, nodeId, roleInstanceName, tenantName,creationTime, virtualMachineUniqueId

| sort by TIMESTAMP  asc nulls last

| where (TIMESTAMP >= datetime(2019-11-04T06:00:00Z) and TIMESTAMP < datetime(2019-11-23T14:50:51Z))

| where virtualMachineUniqueId =="5d141620-2704-4a1f-a804-5f099b576cc7"
