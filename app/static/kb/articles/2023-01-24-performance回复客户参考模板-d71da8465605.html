Hi Arunkumar/Vettri,

Hope you are well. This is MK from Microsoft Azure Support, serving as the escalation resource for 21v Support team.

I understand that you have been repeatedly experiencing network instability on the China Azure – US connection, and I hereby apologize for the inconvenience and impact this might have caused to your business. As you have heard a lot from these support ticket, overseas performance issue has been a real pain for a fairly long period, which results from a mix of facts including Great Firewall as well as public internet qualify that is beyond Azure’s control.

After a series of pushing on China Telecom (ISP in China) and escalation, we acquired the below investigation result (“The packet drop being caused by the congestion between China Telecom (China ISP) and Cogent (your local ISP).”). You may not experience any congestion connecting domestically or even connecting with areas other than China; However, the bandwidth and quality of the peer connection depends on both sides – both China Telecom and Cogent – and their mutual deal. While we are making efforts to push China Telecom side for bandwidth expansion, it can help a lot or at least benefit the investigation if you could help to raise your concerns to your local ISP as well. Hope this makes sense.

On the other hand, I have to say, regretfully, that based on my experience, bandwidth expansion (no matter which side we are talking about) would not be something quick and easy even if the concerned ISPs commit to make a change. The sad truth is, it’s beyond our control despite the continuous requests and complaints we make on behalf of our customer to these ISPs. As I understand that your business is suffering from such instability, I would personally suggest you to consider 3rd party solutions, who focus on accelerating network speed and providing more stable overseas connection. For instance Aryaka is one of the popular ones on the market. You may to reach out to them to know more details or check on other vendors if you would. Please note that this is entirely 3rd party solution and out of Azure scope.

Lastly, I’m sincerely sorry about the trouble you are going through. Please let me know if you have any question about the above.

Thanks!

Best regards,

MK Wang
