1. Azure Arc Proxy

2. Guest Configuration Extension Service

1.AMAExtHealthMonitor.exe

Computer\HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\AzureMonitorAgent\Secrets and remove the key "persistenceKeyCreated"

C:\Resources\Directory\AMA*
